Lentil Soup

← Back to Recipes

About This Recipe

This homemade lentil soup is a warm, filling meal made with vegetables, flavorful spices, and protein-rich lentils. Its combination of simple ingredients makes it a nutritious addition to a balanced diet.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 45 minutes

Total Time: 55 minutes

Servings: 6


Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 large carrot, cut into small pieces
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 cups dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 6 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1½ teaspoons paprika
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Optional Toppings

  • Fresh parsley
  • Warm crusty bread

Instructions

  1. Place a large pot on the stove and warm the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, stirring until they become fragrant and slightly softened.
  2. Mix in the carrots and celery. Allow the vegetables to cook for several minutes until they begin to soften.
  3. Pour in the rinsed lentils, tomatoes, and broth. Sprinkle in the cumin, coriander, and paprika, then add the bay leaves.
  4. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil. Lower the heat, cover the pot, and let everything cook for approximately 35–40 minutes, or until the lentils are tender.
  5. Take out the bay leaves. For a thicker texture, blend a portion of the soup and stir it back into the pot.
  6. Finish by mixing in the lemon zest and juice. Season with salt and pepper according to your preference.
  7. Ladle the soup into bowls and add fresh parsley on top. Serve while warm.

Why It's Good for Iron

Lentils contain non-heme iron, making them a helpful plant-based food for people looking to include more iron in their meals. They also provide protein and dietary fiber. Adding lemon juice supplies vitamin C, which improves the body's ability to absorb iron from plant foods.


Storage Tips

Allow any remaining soup to cool before transferring it into a sealed container. Keep it refrigerated and enjoy within 3–5 days. You can also freeze individual portions for convenient meals later.
