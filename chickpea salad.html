Avocado & Chickpea Salad

← Back to Recipes

About This Recipe

This colorful avocado and chickpea salad combines creamy avocado, juicy tomatoes, and protein-rich chickpeas with a refreshing lemon dressing. It is an easy, no-cook meal that provides plant-based iron, fiber, and healthy fats.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 0 minutes

Total Time: 20 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups grape tomatoes, cut in half
  • ½ medium red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 ripe avocados, diced

Lemon Dressing

  • 4 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Open the cans of chickpeas and rinse them thoroughly under cool water. Drain away any excess liquid.
  2. Wash the tomatoes and cut them into halves. Thinly slice the red onion and dice the avocados.
  3. In a small bowl, combine the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Stir until evenly mixed.
  4. Place the chickpeas, tomatoes, red onion, and avocado into a large serving bowl.
  5. Pour the lemon dressing over the vegetables and chickpeas.
  6. Gently mix everything together so the ingredients are coated without crushing the avocado.
  7. Serve immediately or refrigerate briefly before serving.

Why It's Good for Iron

Chickpeas are a source of non-heme iron, which is the type of iron found in plant-based foods. They also provide protein and fiber, making this salad a satisfying meal.

Tomatoes and lemon juice contribute vitamin C, which helps the body absorb iron from chickpeas more effectively. Avocados also provide beneficial fats and several vitamins and minerals.

Helpful Tips

  • Choose ripe but firm avocados so they hold their shape when mixed.
  • Use freshly squeezed lemon juice for a brighter flavor.
  • Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container.
  • Add fresh parsley or cilantro for extra flavor.
  • Serve with whole-grain pita or toast for a more filling meal.
