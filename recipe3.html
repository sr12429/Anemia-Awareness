Berry Chocolate Iron Smoothie

← Back to Recipes

About This Recipe

This refreshing berry smoothie combines sweet strawberries, blueberries, banana, and nutrient-rich spinach with a hint of cocoa powder. It is an easy breakfast or snack that provides vitamins, antioxidants, and plant-based iron.

Preparation Time: 5 minutes

Total Time: 5 minutes

Servings: 1–2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh strawberries, stems removed
  • ½ cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 1 cup milk, orange juice, or water
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3–4 ice cubes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wash the strawberries, blueberries, and spinach thoroughly. Remove the strawberry stems and peel the banana.
  2. Add the strawberries, blueberries, banana, and spinach to a blender.
  3. Pour in your choice of milk, orange juice, or water. Add the cocoa powder.
  4. Blend all the ingredients until the mixture becomes smooth and creamy.
  5. Add a few ice cubes if you prefer a colder smoothie. Blend again until everything is combined.
  6. Pour into a glass and enjoy immediately!

Why It's Good for Iron

Spinach contains non-heme iron, a type of iron found in plant-based foods. Cocoa powder and bananas also contribute small amounts of iron to the smoothie.

Strawberries and blueberries provide vitamin C and other beneficial plant compounds. Vitamin C from strawberries helps the body absorb iron from spinach more effectively.

For an extra boost of vitamin C, choose orange juice as your smoothie base. If you prefer milk or water, the smoothie will still provide plenty of nutrients.

Helpful Tips

  • Use frozen berries for a thicker, colder consistency.
  • Choose unsweetened cocoa powder for a rich chocolate flavor.
  • Add more liquid if the smoothie is too thick.
  • Use orange juice to add vitamin C and support iron absorption.
  • Drink soon after blending for the best flavor and texture.
